## Introduction

Welcome to the Web Screen Recorder! This application allows you to record your screen directly from your web browser without installing any additional software. Whether you need to create tutorials, record presentations, or capture gameplay, Web Screen Recorder makes it simple and convenient.

### Key Features
- Record your screen with high quality
- Capture microphone audio
- Pause and resume recordings
- Save recordings to your device
- Manage your recording library
- Adjust video quality settings

## Getting Started

### System Requirements
- A modern web browser (Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge)
- Internet connection (for initial loading only)
- Microphone (optional, for audio recording)

### Accessing the Application
1. Open your web browser
2. Navigate to the Web Screen Recorder URL or open the HTML file locally
3. The application will load in your browser window

### Interface Overview
The Web Screen Recorder has a clean, intuitive interface with three main sections:
- **Recorder Tab**: Where you record your screen
- **Settings Tab**: Where you configure recording options
- **Recordings Tab**: Where you manage your saved recordings

## Recording Your Screen

### Starting a Recording
1. Navigate to the **Recorder** tab
2. Click the **Start Recording** button
3. Select what you want to record:
   - Entire Screen
   - Application Window
   - Browser Tab
4. Click **Share** to begin recording

### Recording Controls
Once recording has started, you'll see the following controls:
- **Pause**: Temporarily pause the recording
- **Resume**: Continue a paused recording
- **Stop**: End the recording session
- **Timer**: Shows the duration of your recording

### Ending a Recording
1. Click the **Stop** button
2. The recording will be processed and displayed in the video player
3. You can now preview, download, or discard the recording

## Managing Recordings

### Viewing Recordings
1. Navigate to the **Recordings** tab
2. You'll see a list of all your saved recordings
3. Each recording shows:
   - Title
   - Date recorded
   - Duration
   - File size

### Playing Recordings
1. Click the **Play** button on any recording
2. The recording will open in the video player
3. Use the video controls to play, pause, or seek through the recording

### Downloading Recordings
1. Find the recording you want to download
2. Click the **Download** button
3. The recording will be saved to your device's default download location

### Deleting Recordings
1. Find the recording you want to delete
2. Click the **Delete** button
3. Confirm that you want to delete the recording
4. The recording will be permanently removed

## Settings and Configuration

### Audio Settings
1. Navigate to the **Settings** tab
2. Under **Audio Source**, select one of the following:
   - **Microphone**: Record audio from your microphone
   - **No Audio**: Record without sound

### Video Quality Settings
1. Navigate to the **Settings** tab
2. Under **Video Quality**, select one of the following:
   - **High (1080p)**: Best quality, larger file size
   - **Medium (720p)**: Balanced quality and file size
   - **Low (480p)**: Smaller file size, lower quality

### Frame Rate Settings
1. Navigate to the **Settings** tab
2. Under **Frame Rate**, select one of the following:
   - **60 fps**: Smooth motion, higher resource usage
   - **30 fps**: Standard motion, balanced resource usage
   - **15 fps**: Lower motion quality, minimal resource usage

## Troubleshooting

### Permission Issues
**Problem**: Browser asks for permission but recording doesn't start.
**Solution**: 
1. Make sure you've granted all necessary permissions
2. Try refreshing the page
3. Check that no other application is using your camera or microphone

### Recording Quality Issues
**Problem**: Recording appears blurry or laggy.
**Solution**:
1. Try a lower video quality setting
2. Reduce the frame rate
3. Close other resource-intensive applications

### Audio Issues
**Problem**: No audio in the recording.
**Solution**:
1. Make sure you've selected "Microphone" in the audio settings
2. Check that your microphone is properly connected
3. Verify that you've granted microphone permissions to the browser

### Storage Issues
**Problem**: Can't save more recordings.
**Solution**:
1. Download and delete older recordings
2. Clear browser cache
3. Free up space on your device

## FAQ

### Q: Is my data secure?
A: Yes. All recordings are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to any server unless you explicitly choose to do so.

### Q: Can I record system audio?
A: Browser limitations currently restrict the ability to record system audio in most browsers. You can record microphone audio only.

### Q: How long can I record for?
A: There is no time limit imposed by the application. However, your browser and device memory may limit very long recordings.

### Q: What format are the recordings saved in?
A: Recordings are saved in WebM format, which is widely supported by modern browsers and media players.

### Q: Can I edit my recordings?
A: The current version does not include editing capabilities. You can use a video editing software after downloading your recording.

### Q: Does this work on mobile devices?
A: The application is designed to be responsive, but screen recording functionality may be limited on mobile browsers due to platform restrictions.
